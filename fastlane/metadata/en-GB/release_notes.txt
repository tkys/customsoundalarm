■ Oversleep guard (Pro, early access)
If you haven't stopped your alarm by the time you choose, it plays the same sound again, louder. If you turn it on during early access, you can keep using it after the full release.

■ Ring now
Tap "Ring now" on the alarm screen to hear your alarm right away, at its real volume.

■ Help when it's too quiet
Alarm volume follows Ringtone and Alerts in Settings. "Too quiet?" shows you where to change it.

■ A simpler alarm screen
Settings are arranged more like the Clock app, and an alarm you edit is turned on when you save it.
