■ Clearer "Test Ring"
One button now shows "Ringing soon" while you wait and "Ringing" while it plays. You can also test from the bottom of "How to make alarms louder".

■ Alarm list
• After you save an alarm or turn it on, the time until it rings appears for a moment.
• Times follow your device's 12-hour or 24-hour setting and are shown larger.
• During "Rest alarms", you can see at a glance which alarms will not ring. One-time alarms stay on.
• The list moves to the alarm you just added so you can see which one it is.

■ Fixes
• Fixed alarms with "Gentle start" sometimes playing a different sound, and sounds longer than 10 minutes not getting louder gradually.
• Fixed the Oversleep guard "Try" button sometimes taking a few minutes to ring.
• Fixed the trim handles being hard to grab when the range covers the full width.
• Closing the editor now asks for confirmation only when you changed something.
• Fixed a rare case where the app could close right after adding a sound from a video.
