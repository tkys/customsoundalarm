■ A short welcome for new users
The first time you open the app, a few screens show how to turn part of a song into an alarm. Then you can start right away: from a video, from an audio file, or with a built-in sound.

■ Alarm permission at a better time
The app now asks for alarm permission after the welcome screens, not the moment it opens.

If you already use the app, nothing changes for you. Thanks for using My Sound Alarm!
