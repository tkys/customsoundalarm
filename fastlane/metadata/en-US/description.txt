Set your favorite music or video audio as your alarm — and hear it in full, not cut short.

KEY FEATURES

Up to 10 Minutes, Played to the End
Custom ringtones on iPhone are capped at about 40 seconds, so your own audio always gets cut off. My Sound Alarm has no such limit. Pull up to 10 minutes out of a video, and audio files have no length limit at all. Your alarm plays all the way through before repeating, so the intro, the build-up, and the part you actually wanted to wake up to are all still there.

Your Own Audio, Not a Catalog
This is for the sounds you already have: a song file, a voice recording, audio pulled from a video you saved. Nothing to subscribe to, no catalog to pick from.

Extract Audio from Videos
Select any part of a video from your Camera Roll and extract the audio. Adjust start and end points while previewing, so you can find the perfect clip.

Audio File Import
Supports MP3, AAC, WAV, and M4A formats. Import directly from the Files app.

Built on Apple's Alarm System
Uses AlarmKit, Apple's official alarm framework. Your alarm rings the way the built-in Clock app does — this is not a background workaround that goes quiet when you need it most.

Share Sheet Integration
Add audio or video directly from other apps using the share sheet.

Repeat Scheduling
Set alarms to repeat on specific days of the week. Weekdays only, weekends only, or any combination.

Snooze
Choose your own snooze interval instead of being stuck with nine minutes.

Night Mode
Turn your iPhone into a bedside clock. Large dimmed display that stays readable in a dark room.

SUPPORTED FORMATS
MP3 / AAC / WAV / M4A / Video (MOV, MP4) audio extraction

NOTES
- Requires iOS 26 or later (AlarmKit)
- Longer sounds use more storage. A 10-minute alarm sound is roughly 50 MB.
