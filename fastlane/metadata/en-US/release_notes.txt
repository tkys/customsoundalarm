■ Skip days
• Swipe right on an alarm, or touch and hold it, then choose "Skip" to skip just the next one.
• Repeating alarms can skip dates you choose.
• Use the button at the top right to skip all alarms for a while.

■ Playlists in one tap
When you have two or more of your own sounds, the Library and the sound picker suggest a playlist that plays a different song each time. Tap it to create one.

■ Settings
Tap the gear at the top left for Night Mode, how-to, contact and reviews.

■ Also
• In the sound picker, tap a row to hear that sound.
• Choose where to import a video from right on the screen.
• Fixed an issue where a one-time alarm with a playlist played the same song every time.
