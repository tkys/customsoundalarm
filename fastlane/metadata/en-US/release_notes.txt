■ Pick the part to ring, right from Share
Share a video or audio file from Photos or Files and choose My Sound Alarm. The trim screen opens when you open the app.

■ Faster alarm setup
• New alarms keep your last Snooze, Playlist and Gentle start settings.
• Above the time, you'll see 5 and 10 minutes after your latest alarm. Tap one to set it.

■ Know when alarms can't ring
If alarm permission is off, the top of the list shows "Alarms won't ring". Tap "Allow" to fix it.

■ Also
• When a quiet sound is made louder to match the others, a short note appears under it.
• When you have two of your own sounds, the app lets you know you can make a playlist.

Thank you for using My Sound Alarm.
