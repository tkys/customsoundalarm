■ Playlists (PRO · Early access)
Group your sounds into a playlist and wake up to it — in order, shuffled, or one random track. You can also set a Gentle start and a Crossfade between tracks. If you start using it during early access, you can keep using it after the full release.

■ Now in 13 languages
My Sound Alarm is now available in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Polish, Czech and Arabic, following your device language.

■ Clearer “Ring now”
If “Ring now” can’t ring — for example, because alarms aren’t allowed yet — the screen now tells you why. Tap “Allow Alarms” to fix it right there.

Thank you for using My Sound Alarm.
