■ Use songs from the Music app
When adding a sound, choose "Add from Music" to pick the part to ring from songs in your Music app.

■ Open from Control Center
Add "My Sound Alarm" and "Add Alarm" to Control Center. Tap to open your alarm list or the add screen.

■ A simpler alarm editor
Snooze, Label and Gentle start are now under "More".

■ Also
• If you pick a part that is nearly silent, the app tells you.
• The email from "Report a bug or ask a question" in Settings now attaches the app's status to help us look into problems. It does not include song names or sound files. You can remove it before sending.
• Fixed overlapping text on the trim screen at the largest text size.
