■ Playlists (PRO · Early access)
Group your sounds into a playlist and wake up to it: in order, shuffled, or one random track. You can also choose how tracks blend into each other, with a fade or a crossfade.

■ Gentle start (PRO · Early access)
The alarm starts quiet and gets louder over 5, 15 or 30 seconds.

If you start using these during early access, you can keep using them after the full release.

■ Library
A new Library tab keeps your sounds and playlists in one place.

■ Now in 13 languages
My Sound Alarm is now available in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Polish, Czech and Arabic, following your device language.

■ Bug fixes
Fixed an issue where a repeating alarm could ring again within the same minute after you stopped it.
Fixed an issue where an alarm that no longer appeared in the app could ring at a time you did not set. After updating, open the app to remove any such alarms.

■ Easier to read
Text colors and contrast have been revised, and screens stay readable with larger text sizes.

■ “Ring now”
“Ring now” now plays the sound you’re editing. If it can’t ring, the screen tells you why and lets you allow alarms right there.

Thank you for using My Sound Alarm.
