How to set part of a song as your iPhone alarm

There are three common ways to wake up to part of a song on iPhone. Here is what each one can do, and the steps it takes.

Clock app

Choose a song from your library. The alarm starts at the beginning of the song.

  1. Open Clock, tap Alarm, then the + button.
  2. Choose Sound and pick a song from your library.
  3. Save the alarm. It plays from the start of the song.

GarageBand

Trim a section and export it as a ringtone. Custom ringtones are up to 30 seconds, and the export takes a few steps.

  1. Create a GarageBand project and import the audio you want to use.
  2. Trim the section you want. A custom ringtone can be up to 30 seconds.
  3. Export it as a ringtone, then choose it as the alarm sound in Clock.

My Sound Alarm

Pick the part you want on a waveform. Audio files can be used at their full length, and videos can be up to 10 minutes.

  1. Open an audio file or a video in the app.
  2. Choose the part you want on the waveform.
  3. Save it as the alarm sound. Alarms use AlarmKit on iOS 26.

How to use the sound from a video

Choose a video from your photo library or the Files app. My Sound Alarm extracts the audio, and you choose the part on the waveform. Videos can be up to 10 minutes. Travel videos, family moments and your pet's voice can become an alarm sound.
