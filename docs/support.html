Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome. Write to tkysdev@gmail.com in any language.

What files can I use?

Audio files in MP3, AAC, WAV or M4A, and videos (MOV, MP4) from your photo library or the Files app. You can also send a file to My Sound Alarm from another app with the Share button. Songs from streaming services such as Apple Music or Spotify are protected and can't be imported.

How long can a sound be?

Up to 10 minutes from a video. Audio files can be imported at their full length. The alarm repeats the part you choose, so 20–30 seconds usually works best.

The alarm didn't ring.

Check that alarms are allowed for the app in Settings > Apps > My Sound Alarm. Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit, so they ring in Silent mode, when a Focus is on and with the app closed. If an alarm still doesn't ring, please email us your iOS version and what happened.

The alarm is too quiet.

Alarm volume follows Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Ringtone and Alerts. The volume in Control Center doesn't affect alarms. On iPhone with Face ID, Attention Aware Features lowers alert volume while you're looking at the screen (Settings > Face ID & Passcode).

Can I turn off vibration?

The app can't change how the alarm vibrates; iPhone controls it. To turn off all vibration, go to Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Vibration. This affects every app.

What is the oversleep guard?

If you haven't stopped an alarm by the time you choose, it rings again with the same sound, made louder. It's a Pro feature in early access. If you start using it during early access, you keep it after the full release.

Requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
